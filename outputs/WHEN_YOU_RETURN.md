# Read this when you return

The software foundation is built and tested. It has 4 ASL choices, 4 Emirati Sign Language choices, improved hand tracking, webcam capture, text, speech, confidence rejection, a controlled reception agent, a separate final-test report and an optional computer-control tab.

For the full beginner explanation, read `docs/FULL_BEGINNER_GUIDE.md`.

A personalized ASL model trained only on Yusuf exists. It is not proof that the system works for new people. No Emirati model exists yet, and Codex cannot honestly create the missing human sign-language data.

## Your next actions

1. Start the app with `Start SignBridge.vbs`; it does not keep PowerShell open.
2. Ask fluent signers to check all 8 linked sign forms.
3. If you are the demonstrator, record 20 to 30 varied attempts per class. Add other training people if available.
4. Add more ASL **Help** and **OTHER / NO SIGN** examples, then retrain the ASL model.
5. Collect Emirati examples and train that model.
6. Test at least one completely new person in **Final test**; two or three is better.
7. Test a complete **SignBridge agent** interaction, including a second sign after a staff reply, and record every correction or false action.
8. Remove any sign that misses the test targets.

Do not call it a full translator. Call it an **8-sign bilingual isolated-sign prototype with a controlled reception agent**. Its local task numbers are not connected to outside services. The computer-control gestures are a separate generic-gesture experiment.
